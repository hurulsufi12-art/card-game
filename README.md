HARBORBOUND is a card game: a title screen, a table with the player's and
opponent's hands, cards with value, suit and cost, and life scores, ending in a
win or loss screen. A match runs 12 rounds on a suit wheel — Ember, Leaf and
Tide, with Stone neutral — against the AI opponent Maro.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html`, press Space or click to start. Each hand is a
decision: play a card, beat the trick, and protect your life. Win by holding a
life advantage when the harbor is decided.
